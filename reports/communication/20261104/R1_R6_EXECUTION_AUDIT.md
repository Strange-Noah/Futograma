## Lifecycle / operational findings
Unchanged repeat does reuse records with zero new resolver calls. Content/dependency change and retry-date behavior exist and are covered by synthetic tests; they are not a complete real-source correction exercise. The input adapter caches source results by supplied source JSON, and reuses parsed/dossier state; mutable archive versions and availability changes need explicit invalidation. Resolved findings with unchanged dependencies have no timed retry. If a prior resolved claim disappears from discovery, no sweep closes/reopens the stale persisted finding. Transition keys encode semantic content rather than predecessor lineage, so a state A→B→A may lose the second A transition through INSERT OR IGNORE. These are code-inspection risks, not observed corruption of the frozen facts.

The adapter's version changes when its coverage/hash list changes; the core fingerprints members, fixtures, statuses and windows. A relevant captured-source change can therefore reopen an encountered claim. This does not prove all relevant changes are discovered. The 500-call budget bounds callbacks, not discovery's day/player/fixture loops or network time. No global wall-time, fetched-byte or broader discovery budget exists in this run.

The generic core loops once over discovery, then re-audits recovered statuses; it does not iterate broad discovery to incorporate newly recovered fixtures. All modes accept the same interface, but the retained adapter ignores mode and only INCREMENTAL was exercised on real data. ONBOARD/BACKFILL/DELAYED behavior is synthetic-only evidence.

## Candidate evidence caveat
R6 exclusion checks derived sheet.players for a canonical player ID and trusts complete_squad/issues. It does not require a target provider alias nor prove all source IDs are normalized before concluding absence. Independent audit found 12/13 candidates absent from their raw squads under known retained aliases, 1/13 without the required alias. No evidence of the target being present was observed in those 12 checks; this does not establish injury, suspension or cause, or independent publisher corroboration. Evidence.valid checks form/locator/hash shape; its origin field is not used to enforce independent corroboration.

Checkpoint 3 validation: the suspected transition-history issue was reproduced in an isolated synthetic in-memory test (3 evaluations, 2 transitions, correct final PENDING state). It is now a demonstrated lifecycle defect, not merely a code-inspection risk. The frozen football reference remains intact.

Additional dataflow finding: R5 ordering is real, but window context is only attached to initial national R4 finding dictionaries. gaps receives no windows argument, the retained adapter supplies no national fixtures, and its resolution methods do not use window context to discover national activity. Recomputed final_opportunities also omit the earlier attachment. Thus stage order alone does not meet the full international-period discovery/audit requirement; this is an implementation/integration gap, not a source proof of no national activity.
