# Audit authority and evidence-calibrated judgment

Accepted facts, roster decisions and identities retain their prior authority within documented scope. An audit can create an investigation obligation or propose a correction; it cannot revoke acceptance because a new adapter, timestamp, certificate or search is incomplete. Only the established evidence-backed adjudication process can authorize a canonical change. The reconstructed audit introduces no canonical writer and changes no production data-quality schema.

| Module | Accepted inputs / detection responsibility | Permitted outputs / escalation / write authority |
|---|---|---|
| R1 integration | Existing on-date roster_policy decisions and retained interval boundaries; source replay is supplemental | Preserve audit eligibility despite replay gaps; separate candidates/challenges. No new membership facts or historical joining dates |
| R2 source consumers | Accepted fixture facts plus SourceService native observations and existing identity bindings | Scoped discovery/evidence candidates, not automatic canonical facts |
| R5 | Typed official calendar observations and verified national fixtures | Calendar/observed context; no nationality-based membership or continuous call-up inference |
| R3/R4 | Scoped accepted eligibility, actual relevant peer fixtures and accepted statuses | Sensitive warnings and material challenges. Retain accepted status as the baseline; no fact mutation |
| R6 | Precise claim, original acceptance, relevant available source evidence, explicit bounds | Reasoned conclusions, candidate explanations, confirmed scoped evidence or unresolved work. Technical retry is distinct from human Pending; no canonical correction authorized |
| Existing adjudication | Affirmative verified new/contradictory evidence meeting the applicable standard | The existing canonical path alone may accept a correction, preserving original fact/provenance/decision/history and rollback |

Every important assessment records claim, examined evidence, evidentiary direction, confidence status, limitations and next action. CONFIRMED, REFUTED, SUPPORTED_BUT_UNCONFIRMED, INDETERMINATE, CONFLICTED, NOT_CHECKED, NOT_IMPLEMENTED and BLOCKED are reporting/evidence classifications. They do not replace existing canonical quality states. No arbitrary numerical probabilities are used.

Evidence is evaluated for relevance to the exact player/fixture/interval, identity reliability, underlying origin, consistency, coverage and sufficiency under existing policy. Shared bodies and known publisher aliases do not create independent corroboration. Unknown syndication remains a stated limitation. Source lookup failure or unexecuted work is not negative football evidence.

Autonomous judgment is required. Existing sufficient accepted evidence can settle an audit question without recertifying it from scratch. Same-role minute variations are evaluated through the existing minute_policy.select rather than a second adjudicator. A complete identity-bound squad or jointly verified history/detail claim can support a scoped affirmative conclusion; absolute certainty or an absence explanation is not required to preserve a valid status. Confirming source evidence still does not authorize a canonical correction.

Human Pending is not the default for uncertainty. Missing adapters, unperformed requests and resource-limited work remain explicit autonomous work/retry states. The current escalation guard requires a remaining material conflict after the relevant bounded plan is completed, rather than simply missing absolute certainty; same-body publications cannot manufacture independence. Cases still open to appropriate autonomous investigation remain in that workflow.

Implementation: reconstruction.engine and RetainedAdapter read accepted SQLite facts/roster policy and emit audit-ledger assessments. Accepted baseline receipts are explicitly non-HTTP references to retained authority, not fabricated source responses. Operational source acquisition/parsing remains through SourceService. Existing production legacy modules and fact writers are unchanged.

Assessment artifacts containing detailed evidence/player references are preserved privately under the isolated project-local run. Only sanitized aggregates are published here. This contract supplements the existing reconstruction; it is not a new pipeline or a full-system recertification mandate.
